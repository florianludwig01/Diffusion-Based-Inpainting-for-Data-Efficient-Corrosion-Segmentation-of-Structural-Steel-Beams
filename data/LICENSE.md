## Sources and synthetic images

The RTest photographs and the six photographs in `synthetic_examples/RSource/` were acquired by the authors.

The synthetic example subset contains 12 images generated using FLUX.1 Fill Dev and 10 images generated using Juggernaut XL v9 (SDXL). All were generated by inpainting the authors' source photographs. No third-party source photographs are included.

The synthetic images contain AI-generated corrosion and should not be interpreted as photographs of actual corrosion.

The corresponding annotations describe corrosion regions in YOLO segmentation format, with class 0 denoting corrosion. They are not the masks used for inpainting.
